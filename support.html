Frequently asked questions

Who can see the games I log?

Your logbook is visible only to accounts that follow you. Following does not require your approval, so anyone can choose to follow you and will then be able to see it. Your username, display name, bio, favorite team, picks, and prediction record are visible to anyone signed in to Chalk. See the Privacy Policy for full detail.

How do I stop someone from seeing my logbook?

You have three controls, and none of them notify the other person:

  • Remove a follower — Friends → Followers → Remove, or from their profile. They lose access immediately but can follow you again.
  • Block — open their profile, tap …, then Block. Any follow between you is removed, neither of you can follow the other, and you disappear from each other across the app.
  • Mute — open their profile, tap …, then Mute. Hides them from your leaderboard and suggestions. They can still follow you and see your logbook, so mute and remove them if you want both.

Manage everyone you have blocked or muted under Profile → Blocked & muted accounts.

How do I report abuse or offensive content?

Email shreyas.bala1@gmail.com with the username and a description of the problem, and a screenshot if you have one. We review every report and remove content that violates our rules. You can block the account in the meantime so you stop seeing them right away.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, profile photos, logged games, reviews, rankings, picks, watchlist, and social connections. It cannot be undone. You can also email us to request deletion.

Do picks involve real money?

No. Picks are predictions only. There is no wagering, no betting odds, no virtual currency, and nothing of value is staked or won. Your record is an accuracy count and a streak, used for the friends leaderboard.

I did not receive my verification email

Check your spam folder first, then use Resend code on the verification screen. Verification codes expire after a short time, so request a fresh one rather than reusing an old email. If it still does not arrive, email us and we will confirm your account manually.

How do I change my profile picture or username?

Tap your photo on the Profile tab to change it, and use Edit Profile for your display name, bio, and favorite team. Usernames are set when you create your account; email us if you need yours changed.

Where do scores and schedules come from?

Game schedules and scores come from publicly available ESPN data. None of your personal information is sent to ESPN. Scores occasionally lag live broadcast by a short interval.
